Scoring Methodology

Our City Health measures 12 civic dimensions using news and social signals.

Step 1: Document Selection

  • Civic Relevance (45%): Keyword matching 12 dimensions.
  • Source Quality (30%): News reputation vs social.
  • Length (15%) & Recency (10%)

Step 2: Fairness

  • Capped at 500 docs/city. Balanced sources.

Step 3: AI Analysis

  • GPT-4 analyzes snippets for a holistic health score (0-100).
  • Scores are NOT weighted averages but integrated assessments.
