SECTION A

Key Summary

Our City Health aims to build the global standard for civic data. We are creating a real-time index that tracks the health of 100+ cities.

Giving decision-makers a clear, comparable score for everything from Housing to Safety.

The Vision

To create a single dashboard where you can compare 100 cities instantly.

The Proof (POC)

We have already built a working prototype (Pipeline v3). It successfully scrapes news and social media, cleans the data, and scores it. It works today.

The Plan

Scale this proven technology to cover the world's top 100 economies.

SECTION B

Problem & Market Context

Governments and companies are making billion-dollar decisions using bad maps.

⚡

They are Slow

Official data (like the Census) is 2 years old. You are trying to solve today's problems with data from 2024.

🧩

They are Incomplete

City-wide averages hide the truth. Knowing "New York is doing okay" masks that specific neighborhoods are failing.

🎯

They are Inaccurate

Traditional "social listening" tools just count keywords. They lack context and can't distinguish debate from risk.

Our City Health 03 / 09

SECTION C

Proposed Solution

We aim to build a factory for city data that fixes these three flaws.

🚀

Fast (Real-Time)

Our engine runs continuously. We capture shifts in sentiment hours after they happen, not years later.

🔍

Complete (Granular)

We don't just look at the city level. We drill down to specific districts and specific topics (like "Housing" vs "Transport").

⚖️

Accurate (Standardized)

We use AI to apply the exact same scoring logic to every city, allowing for a true "apples-to-apples" comparison.
