About Our City Health

Our City Health measures affordability, services, safety, opportunity, culture, environment, transportation, governance, housing, economy, education, and health across the world’s largest cities using news and social signals.

We emphasize transparency and fairness: each city’s analysis balances document counts, source diversity, recency, and relevance. All citations are preserved so results can be verified.

Scoring Methodology

Step 1: Document Selection Scoring

Each article/post is scored using a weighted formula to determine which documents are most valuable for analysis:

  • 45% - Civic Relevance: Keyword density matching 12 civic dimensions (affordability, services, safety, opportunity, culture, environment, transportation, governance, housing, economy, education, health). Score: min(hits/8.0, 1.0)
  • 30% - Source Quality/Reputation: News sources get reputation scores (0.60-0.95), Reddit posts get 0.58, other sources get 0.52
  • 15% - Length/Coverage: Longer articles (up to 600 words) score higher. Formula: min(word_count/600.0, 1.0)
  • 10% - Recency: Recent content scores higher: ≤14 days (1.0), ≤30 days (0.9), ≤90 days (0.7), ≤180 days (0.55), ≤365 days (0.45), >365 days (0.35)

Final Selection Score: (0.30 × quality) + (0.45 × relevance) + (0.15 × length) + (0.10 × recency)

Step 2: Fairness Normalization

To ensure no city is advantaged by sheer volume, we apply strict fairness caps:

  • Per-City Document Cap: Maximum 500 documents per city (configurable via --city_docs flag)
  • Source Type Balance: Documents are evenly distributed across source types (news articles, Reddit posts, Reddit comments)
  • Domain Diversity: Maximum documents per domain = max(3, city_docs ÷ max(8, number_of_domains))
  • Title Deduplication: Duplicate titles are filtered out to avoid redundancy

Step 3: AI Scoring (OpenAI GPT Model)

The selected documents are formatted as snippets and sent to OpenAI's language model for analysis:

  • Model: GPT-4 or GPT-3.5-turbo (configurable)
  • Temperature: 0.2 (low for consistent, focused results)
  • Input Format: Up to 100 snippets per city, each containing: Title (20 words), Text (60 words), Source, URL
  • Output Structure:
    • Overall Health Score: Single integer 0-100 (holistic assessment, not a weighted average)
    • 12 Dimension Scores: Each dimension receives a score (0-100) and rationale
    • Top 10 Issues: Array of issues with name and "why it matters" explanation
  • Scoring Guidance: Higher scores indicate better civic health (net of sentiment). The model weighs recency (implied), quality/reputation, length, and diversity across sources (news vs social). It favors specificity (policies, programs, metrics) over generic text.

Step 4: Final Scores

The overall health score (0-100) is produced directly by the AI model as a holistic assessment of all 12 dimensions combined. It is not a weighted average of dimension scores, but rather the model's integrated evaluation of the city's overall civic health based on all available signals.

Transparency & Auditability

Every city exposes all source links used (Articles + Reddit posts) for full auditability. Cities with limited data are marked by lower coverage; we never fabricate citations. All citations are preserved in the JSON output and displayed in the sidebar when you click on a city.
